Who Can Join

Any currently enrolled CU Boulder student. You don't need to be in ATLAS or engineering, that's kind of the point of the lab.

Orientation

Before you can use most of the equipment you need a general orientation, and separate trainings for the woodshop, laser, and CNC. Orientations run most weeks — check the calendar below.
