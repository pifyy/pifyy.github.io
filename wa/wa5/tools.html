Tools & Equipment

Here's roughly what's on the floor. Some of it needs a quick training before you can touch it — see Get Access.

3D Printing

A row of FDM printers plus a couple resin printers for the finer detail stuff.

Woodshop

Table saw, miter saw, drill press — a full woodshop, but you need the safety training first.

Laser Cutting

A couple laser cutters for engraving and cutting flat stock. Great for prototypes and signage.

Electronics

Soldering stations, oscilloscopes, and a stock of common components if you're building circuits.

Textiles

Sewing machines, an embroidery machine, and a heat press for wearable or fabric projects.

CNC

A CNC router for cutting bigger pieces than the laser can handle. Training required.
