About BTU Lab

Our Mission

Basically, give students a place to build things they couldn't build in a normal classroom, and don't gatekeep it behind a specific major.

Our Values

  • Hands-on learning over lectures about learning.
  • Everyone starts somewhere — beginners are welcome, not tolerated.
  • Shared tools work because people take care of them.

Anti-Harassment Policy

Harassment of any kind isn't allowed in the lab, online, or at lab events, and it'll get you removed. If something happens, tell a staff member or reach out on the People & Contact page.
