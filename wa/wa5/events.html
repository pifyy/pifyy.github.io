Events & Community

Workshops

Short skill-building sessions, usually an hour or two, on stuff like soldering basics or how not to break the laser cutter.

Open Hack Nights

Every Thursday night the lab stays open late and staff hang around if you get stuck on something.

Classes

A few ATLAS classes actually meet in the lab, mostly the fabrication and prototyping ones. Ask your professor if yours is one of them.

Projects

Student project teams can reserve bench space for a semester — capstones, club projects, whatever. Talk to staff about setting that up.
